Quick answers

Do I need an Apple Watch? No. The journal and AI reflections work on any supported iPhone. An Apple Watch adds detailed sleep staging (REM, deep, core) for richer insights.

Where is my data? On your device, synced only to your own private iCloud. Your health data never leaves your phone. See our Privacy Policy.

Are the insights medical advice? No. Turiya is for reflection and wellness, not diagnosis or treatment.

How do I manage Health access? In the iOS Settings app under Privacy & Security → Health → Turiya.
