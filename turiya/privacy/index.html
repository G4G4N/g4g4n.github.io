Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Turiya is built so your most personal data stays on your device. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Your dreams are stored on your device and backed up only to your own private iCloud via Apple CloudKit. We cannot read them.
  • Your health data (sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate) is read from Apple Health on your device only. It is never uploaded, shared, sold, or sent to any AI model or server.
  • AI interpretations run on your device using Apple's on-device intelligence. Only the text of a dream is ever processed by a model — never your health data.
  • We do not collect, see, or sell any of your data. Turiya has no accounts and no analytics that identify you.

Apple Health

Turiya requests read-only access to specific Health data types (sleep analysis, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, respiratory rate) to notice patterns between your sleep and your dreams. This processing happens entirely on your device. We never write to Health, and your health data is never transmitted off your device. You can grant or revoke this access at any time in the iOS Settings app. Turiya is fully usable with no Health access at all.

Artificial intelligence

When you ask for an interpretation, the text of that dream may be processed by Apple's on-device foundation models (including Apple's Private Cloud Compute, which Apple operates under its own privacy protections). No health data is ever included in any AI request. Interpretations are reflective, not medical or clinical advice.

iCloud sync

If you are signed into iCloud, your dreams sync across your devices through your private CloudKit database. This is your personal iCloud storage, governed by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. We have no access to it.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data.
  • We never use your health data for advertising.
  • We never make medical or clinical claims; insights are reflections, not diagnoses.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Turiya is intended for users 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Use the app with no Health access (journal + AI only).
  • Delete any dream at any time; deletions sync to your iCloud.
  • Manage Health permissions in iOS Settings.

Changes

We will update this policy as the app evolves and revise the date above.
